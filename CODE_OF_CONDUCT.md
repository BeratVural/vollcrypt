# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

Vollcrypt aims to provide a professional, welcoming environment for users and contributors regardless of experience level, background, identity, or viewpoint.

## Expected Behavior

* Be respectful and focus criticism on ideas and code.
* Ask questions in good faith and provide enough context for others to help.
* Accept review feedback professionally.
* Protect private information and follow the security reporting policy.
* Give credit for ideas, testing, documentation, and code.

## Unacceptable Behavior

Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, threats, deliberate disruption, doxxing, and publishing another person's private information are not tolerated.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to berat.vural.tr@gmail.com. Reports will be reviewed confidentially. Maintainers may edit or remove content, close discussions, reject contributions, or restrict participation when necessary to protect the community.

## Scope

This code applies in the repository, issue tracker, pull requests, Discussions, and other spaces where someone represents the Vollcrypt project.
